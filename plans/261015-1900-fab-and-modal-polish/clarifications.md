- Q: FAB behaviour? → A (Figma wins over the literal wording): click on the FAB (`widget-button`, `app/(main)/_components/widget-button.tsx`, rendered in `app/(main)/layout.tsx` on every main page) opens the quick-action group of frame Sv7DFwBw1h: "Thể lệ" (A), "Viết KUDOS" (B), red round "×" Hủy (C) that closes the group. - Q: "Viết KUDOS" target? → A (Recommended): on /sun-kudos it opens the existing Write Kudo modal directly (same gating as the compose pill: disabled/no-op when the viewer has no sunner; focus returns to the FAB on close). On any other main page it navigates to `/sun-kudos?compose=1`, and the board opens the modal once on load (then removes the param with router.replace so refresh/back does not reopen it).
- Q: "Thể lệ" target? → A (Recommended): there is no rules page yet (MoMorph b1Filzi9i6 not implemented), so it opens the existing "Coming soon" modal pattern already used by deferred pills. No new route.
- Q: Backdrop click? → A (user): clicking/tapping the mask outside the card closes the modal and discards the draft, same as Hủy (supersedes F007 FR-224 / clarification "backdrop does nothing"). Still ignored while submitting (same as Esc). A drag that starts inside the card and ends on the mask must NOT close it (use pointerdown+click on the mask itself).
- Q: Scroll? → A (Recommended): the title stays at the top and the Hủy/Gửi footer stays at the bottom of the card; only the field area scrolls (`overflow-y-auto`, `overscroll-behavior: contain`, smooth momentum on touch). The page behind is scroll-locked while the modal is open (no scroll chaining; restore position and avoid layout shift from the scrollbar). Inner dropdowns (recipient list, hashtag panel, mention list) must stay visible/usable inside the scroll area.
